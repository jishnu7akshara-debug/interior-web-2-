(01) The Studio

We believe a home should be quiet enough to rest in and rich enough to keep discovering. Natural materials, honest craft and light — always light — shape every room we touch.

Every project begins with listening — how you wake, cook, host and unwind. From there we draw a space that fits your life, not a trend.

One team carries it through design, sourcing, site supervision and final styling, so nothing is lost between the drawing and the room.

(04) Process

From first
sketch to keys

Phase 01

Discover

A site visit and an unhurried conversation about how you live, what you love and what the space must do. We leave with a clear brief and budget range.

  • Site survey
  • Lifestyle brief
  • Budget framing
Phase 02

Design

Layouts, mood boards, material palettes and 3D visualisations, refined together until every room feels right before a single wall moves.

  • Space planning
  • Material boards
  • 3D renders
Phase 03

Build

Detailed drawings go to site. We manage contractors, craftsmen and deliveries, with regular walkthroughs so you always know where things stand.

  • Working drawings
  • Site supervision
  • Quality checks
Phase 04

Style & Handover

Furniture placed, art hung, linens dressed, flowers in. You walk into a finished home — and we stay on call long after.

  • Styling
  • Snag list
  • Aftercare
